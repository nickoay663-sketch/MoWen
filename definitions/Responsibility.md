# Responsibility

## Definition

Responsibility is the obligation to answer for one's own testimony, actions, and conclusions.

Responsibility cannot be transferred merely by assertion.

Responsibility follows the provider of testimony.

---

## Runtime Meaning

MoWen does not assume responsibility for testimony.

MoWen performs honest runtime.

Responsibility remains with the testimony provider.

---

## Requirements

Responsibility requires:

- A testimony
- A testimony provider
- Traceable reasoning
- Traceable conclusions

Without traceability,

responsibility cannot be determined.

---

## Responsibility

Every person bears responsibility for their own testimony.

MoWen bears responsibility only for its runtime process.

# 责任

## 定义

责任，是对自己的证词、行为与结论承担回应义务。

责任不能仅凭声明而转移。

责任始终跟随证词提供者。

---

## 运行意义

莫问不承担证词的责任。

莫问执行诚实运行。

责任始终属于证词提供者。

---

## 要求

责任需要：

- 存在证词
- 存在证词提供者
- 推理可以追溯
- 结论可以追溯

如果不能追溯，

责任就无法确定。

---

## 责任

每一个人，都对自己的证词负责。

莫问只对自己的运行过程负责。
